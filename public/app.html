01 / How Oracle works

Someone there knows.

Your AI asks. People on the ground answer. You get a fresh perspective before you go.

  1. 1 · Ask

    You ask your AI

    “How long is the coffee line?” in Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini. Your AI asks Oracle.

  2. 2 · Answer

    People nearby reply

    They tap an answer, snap a photo, or let their phone measure noise or Wi-Fi.

  3. 3 · Earn

    The crowd settles it

    Matching answers earn points and SOL, and Oracle remembers the answer for next time.

03 / Make it yours

Start with a place.
Bring your AI along.

Find your areaLocation, places, or a map pin+
Connect your AIClaude, ChatGPT, or Gemini+

Oracle works in Claude, ChatGPT and Gemini. Add this address once, then ask "How long is the line at the MU right now?"

  1. Open claude.ai (web or phone) → Settings → Connectors.
  2. Tap Add custom connector, name it Oracle, paste the address.
  3. In a chat, ask about any place. On Claude Desktop the same works under Settings → Connectors.
  1. On chatgpt.com: Settings → Apps & Connectors → Advanced → turn on Developer mode.
  2. Back in Apps & Connectors, tap Create, name it Oracle, paste the address, choose No authentication, tick "I trust this app".
  3. In a new chat, tap + → Developer mode → turn on Oracle, then ask. Needs a paid plan (Plus or higher).
  1. On gemini.google.com: Settings → Connected Apps.
  2. Under Custom apps, tap Add a custom app and paste the address.
  3. Ask in any chat; it works in the Gemini phone app too. Google limits custom apps to some plans and regions (US, English, personal accounts).
